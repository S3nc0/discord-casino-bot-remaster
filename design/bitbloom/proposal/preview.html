Bitbloom theme re-tune: preview

Left: the tokens in the app today. Right: the proposal. Same components, same pixel structure (square 2px notched edges, hard offset shadows, stepped motion). Only colour, edges and a few component choices differ. Nothing here is wired into the app.

The proposal: calmer surfaces, one primary (sea teal), one accent (coin gold), quiet slate for supporting fills, borders as tonal steps. Text and meaningful borders keep their contrast.
